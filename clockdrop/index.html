About this app

Clock Drop Puzzle is a falling-piece puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android. Drop round clocks onto the board, link their hands, and close the links into a loop to clear it.

Information we handle

We (the developer) do not collect, store or receive any personal information. There is no account or sign-up, and your score stays on your device.

  • No account or sign-up
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • When you share your score, the text is passed to the app you choose (such as LINE or X). Nothing is sent to us.

Ads (version 1.1 and later)

From version 1.1, a full-screen ad may appear when you tap "Play again". The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general location, and ad interactions. On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is used for personalized ads only if you allow tracking. In regions that require it (such as the EEA and the UK), you will be asked for consent first.

  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ads are rated for all ages. You can turn ads off for good with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase. If you change devices, tap "Restore purchase".

Children

The app can be played by people of any age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the ads shown are limited to content suitable for all ages.

How to play

  • Tap the lowest empty cell in a column to drop the clock there.
  • Tap a higher cell to slide the clock sideways.
  • Tap the falling clock to turn it 90°.
  • A long hand links to the neighboring clock it points at. Short hands link when two short hands face each other.
  • When the links close into a loop, it disappears along with the clocks inside.
  • Each time you drop a clock, every clock on the board moves ahead 1 hour 30 minutes.

このアプリについて

Clock Drop Puzzle は、iPhone・iPad・Android 向けの落ちものパズルゲームです。丸い時計を盤面に落として針をつなげ、線で輪を作ると消えます。

  • アカウントの登録はありません

  • Google のサービスを使うアプリでの情報の使われ方：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

広告は全年齢向けの内容に限っています。買い切りの「広告を消す」を購入すると、広告は出なくなります。機種変更のあとは「購入を復元」を押してください。

年齢を問わず遊べます。お子さまの個人情報を意図して取得することはありません。広告も全年齢向けの内容に限っています。

あそびかた

  • 列のいちばん下の空きマスをタップすると、時計がそこに落ちます。
  • 上のマスをタップすると、時計が横に動きます。
  • 落ちてくる時計をタップすると、90度回せます。
  • 長い針は、指している先のとなりの時計とつながります。短い針は、短い針どうしが向き合うとつながります。
  • つながった線で輪ができると、中の時計ごと消えます。
  • 時計を1つ落とすたびに、盤面の時計がぜんぶ1時間半進みます。
